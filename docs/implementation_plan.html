  • userId — who performed the action

🤖 Phase 3 — n8n AI Automation Workflows

Workflow 1: Payment Reminder

Stepn8n NodeWhat it does
1Webhook (POST)Receives invoice + client + user data from Express
2Google GeminiAI generates a polite, personalized payment reminder email
3GmailSends the reminder email to the client
4HTTP RequestCalls Express callback to update invoice status to "sent"
5Respond to WebhookReturns success message to Express

Workflow 2: Invoice Sender

Stepn8n NodeWhat it does
1Webhook (POST)Receives full invoice data with line items
2Google GeminiAI generates a professional invoice email with itemized breakdown
3GmailSends the invoice email to the client
4HTTP RequestCalls Express callback to update status from "draft" → "sent"
5Respond to WebhookReturns success message to Express
